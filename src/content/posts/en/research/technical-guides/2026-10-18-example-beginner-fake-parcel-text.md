## What happened

You get a text. It says a parcel could not be delivered and you must pay a small fee within 24 hours. The message has a short link. Plenty of people are waiting for a parcel, so it looks believable.

This is a kind of [phishing](/en/glossary/#phishing) sent by SMS, called [smishing](/en/glossary/#smishing). The link leads to a fake page that asks for your card details.

## Why it matters

The fee is small, so people stop worrying. The page is the real target: it collects your card number and often asks you to approve a payment in your banking app. That approval can move far more than the fee.

## What to do

1. Do not tap the link. If you did, close the page and do not type anything.
2. Open the courier's official app, or type its address yourself, and look for the parcel there.
3. If you entered card details, call your bank now, using the number on the back of your card.
4. Keep the text. Report it to the police and to your mobile operator.
