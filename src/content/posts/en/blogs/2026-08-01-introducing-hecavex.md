HECAVEX is an independent publication for cyber threat intelligence and digital investigations. It begins with a simple premise: indicators matter, but the relationships around them usually matter more.

The publication is created and edited by **Deividas Lis**, a cyber threat intelligence researcher, former cybercrime investigator, threat hunter, public speaker and media commentator. HECAVEX is the publication identity. Deividas is the person accountable for its research, judgements and corrections.

## What this publication will examine

The work will follow infrastructure, behaviour and the human systems behind phishing, fraud, malware, influence operations and other digital threats. Some publications will be long investigations; others will be short intelligence notes that preserve a useful observation without inflating it into a campaign.

<aside class="hx-callout key-finding"><strong>Editorial commitment</strong>Confirmed facts, reported claims and analytical assessments will be labelled as such. Confidence will not be implied through tone alone.</aside>

## Evidence and uncertainty

An investigation can be technically detailed and still have limits. HECAVEX will document those limits, distinguish attribution from infrastructure overlap, and update material conclusions when new evidence changes them.

## Four publication classes

The archive is divided by editorial purpose rather than by age:

- **Primary research** presents first-party collection or technical analysis of infrastructure, code, incidents and other artefacts.
- **Technical assessments** explain a technical problem, evaluate an incident or document a reproducible practitioner method.
- **Commentary** presents an argument or analytical position without pretending to be original evidence collection.
- **Signal Briefs** provide short, time-bounded assessments of current developments.

Primary research and technical assessments use a standard record covering evidence basis, methods, scope, limitations, confidence, revision history and citation. Standalone data or code is linked when it can be published safely. When it cannot, the article says so.

## Two languages, one body of research

English and Lithuanian publications share one taxonomy but remain editorially complete in their own language. A translation will link directly to its counterpart. Where no translation exists, the original remains visible without pretending that machine output is an editorial translation.

The scope is not limited to one geography. HECAVEX follows cybercrime infrastructure, malware, espionage, fraud, information operations and defensive tradecraft wherever the evidence leads.
