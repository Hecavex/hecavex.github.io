## Cookies and local preferences

HECAVEX does not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels or session recording. The optional aggregate audience measurement described below sets no HECAVEX cookie.

When you choose a language or visual theme, the site stores these preferences in your browser's local storage:

- `hecavex-language` remembers whether you selected English or Lithuanian.
- `theme` remembers your light, dark or system-theme preference.
- `hecavex-privacy-notice` remembers that you dismissed the site privacy notice.

These values provide the interface settings you explicitly request. They remain in your browser, are not used to identify or track you, and are not transmitted to HECAVEX. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data for `hecavex.com` in your browser. Removing them only resets the corresponding preferences.

The notice displayed at the bottom of the site is informational. It does not ask you to accept advertising or analytics cookies because none are set.

## External links

Articles may link to external sources. HECAVEX does not load tracking technology from those websites merely because a link appears on a page. If you follow an external link, the destination website applies its own privacy and cookie practices.

If HECAVEX later introduces non-essential tracking or third-party embeds, this notice will be updated and any consent required by applicable law will be requested before those technologies are activated.

## Anonymous audience measurement

HECAVEX can use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page use and performance. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies, create a HECAVEX user account, or store an analytics identifier in the browser. The measurement loader is not activated when the browser sends a Do Not Track preference.

Cloudflare may process request information needed to produce aggregate statistics, including the requested page, approximate network and device information, referrer and performance measurements. HECAVEX uses the resulting aggregates to identify useful research, broken journeys and slow pages, not to build individual visitor profiles.
