Deividas Lis is a Vilnius-based cyber threat intelligence researcher, former cybercrime investigator and public speaker. He explains technically complex threats in direct language for security professionals, executives, journalists and general audiences.

For speaking or media enquiries, email **[info@hecavex.com](mailto:info@hecavex.com)** or contact Deividas through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deilis).

## Speaking topics

- Cyber threat intelligence that supports decisions rather than producing longer IOC lists
- OSINT and infrastructure pivoting for fraud, phishing and cybercrime investigations
- Social engineering, ClickFix and why attackers increasingly target people instead of vulnerabilities
- Identity attacks, session theft and why MFA is not a complete security strategy
- Information operations, disinformation infrastructure and AI-assisted influence campaigns
- Intelligence sharing, MISP and practical collaborative defence

Talks can be adapted for conference keynotes, technical sessions, executive briefings, panels, webinars, podcasts and newsroom interviews.

## Media commentary

Deividas is available for concise expert commentary on active cybercrime campaigns, online fraud, data breaches, phishing and social engineering, threat intelligence, OSINT and information operations. Commentary can be provided in **English or Lithuanian**.
