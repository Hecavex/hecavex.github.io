# Rights and reuse boundary

HECAVEX Research combines software, authored publications, research packages and third-party evidence. They do not all share one licence.

## Website software

Original website code and templates in `src/`, `scripts/` and the original interface assets are licensed under the repository's [MIT License](../LICENSE). The MIT grant applies to software only; it does not turn the publication or its evidence archive into a website template or relicense the material described below.

## Authored publication text

Unless an individual page says otherwise, text written by Deividas Lis and carrying the visible article licence is available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution should identify the author, article title, canonical HECAVEX URL and any material changes. `CITATION.cff` describes this citable editorial work, not the software licence.

Translations authored for the paired English and Lithuanian editions follow the same page-level term. A quotation or citation does not acquire broader rights than the source material it reproduces.

## Data and research packages

Downloadable datasets, code samples and evidence bundles use the terms shipped inside their own package. The package-level `README`, `LICENSE` or `LICENSE.md` is authoritative for that release. If no reuse term is stated, this repository grants none by implication.

## Material not relicensed

Third-party reports, quotations, screenshots, product names, logos, captured web content and other evidence remain subject to their existing rights. Their presence in an investigation does not place them under MIT or CC BY 4.0. HECAVEX names, marks and original brand artwork are likewise outside the MIT software grant unless a specific asset says otherwise.

The self-hosted Inter and IBM Plex Mono font files retain the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and their upstream notices in `public/assets/fonts/`.

For a reuse case not resolved by these boundaries, contact HECAVEX before assuming permission.
