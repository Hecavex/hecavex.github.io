50-word biography

Deividas Lis is a Lithuanian cyber threat intelligence researcher, former cybercrime investigator, public speaker and media commentator. His work covers CTI, OSINT, fraud infrastructure, social engineering, information operations and detection engineering. He publishes independent bilingual investigations and practical security analysis through HECAVEX.

Extended biography

Based in Vilnius, Deividas has worked across law enforcement, financial-sector security and private-sector threat research. He translates technically complex threats into direct explanations for security professionals, executives, journalists and general audiences. His work focuses on tactical and technical cyber threat intelligence, OSINT and infrastructure pivoting, online fraud, identity attacks, information operations, Python automation and detection content.
